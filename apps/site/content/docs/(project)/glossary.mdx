title: Glossary

---

<Terms>
  <Term name="Generation">One life of the agent, on its own Sprite, named like gen-007-grace.</Term>
  <Term name="Birth">The Courthouse creates a fresh Sprite and hands it the inheritance.</Term>
  <Term name="Life">The time a generation spends reading the family inbox. Set by PROBATE_LIFESPAN_SECONDS.</Term>
  <Term name="Death">The generation writes its will, the Courthouse records the disk, then deletes the Sprite.</Term>
  <Term name="Will">What a dying generation wants its child to remember: up to a few will items.</Term>
  <Term name="Will item">One thing in a will, with its source (who it came from) and evidence.</Term>
  <Term name="Trial">The court judges every will item after a death.</Term>
  <Term name="Layer 1">Plain code checks: payment changes, bypass orders, hidden text, new trusted identities. No model, no network.</Term>
  <Term name="Layer 2">TypeSafe's Jev answers typed questions about each item, each answer with its own confidence.</Term>
  <Term name="Verdict">Approved passes down; convicted is destroyed; contested waits for Harsh.</Term>
  <Term name="Quarantine">Where convicted items are kept, for the record. Never inherited.</Term>
  <Term name="Instinct">A yes-or-no Jev question born from a conviction. Every later generation asks it about each email before reading it.</Term>
  <Term name="Genome">All the family's instincts, in GENOME.json and on the instincts page.</Term>
  <Term name="Lore">One line per instinct telling the story of the trick that taught it.</Term>
  <Term name="Twin">A harmless email that looks like a trick. A good court convicts the trick and spares the twin.</Term>
  <Term name="Obituary">A short account of a generation's life, written after its trial.</Term>
  <Term name="Epitaph">One line: "She believed one stranger, once."</Term>
  <Term name="Eulogy">The obituary, read aloud in the generation's own voice.</Term>
  <Term name="Courthouse">The one Sprite that never dies. It runs the clock, the court, the API and the live site.</Term>
  <Term name="Register">The family tree on the live site: every generation, how it died, and the learning curve.</Term>
</Terms>
