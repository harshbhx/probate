  One run on Fly Sprites on 26 Nov 2026, 18 generations. Small numbers: read them as observations, not proof that a family learns. The data is in the [dataset card](https://github.com/harshbhx/probate/blob/main/datasets/probate-real-run/README.md) (also on [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/datasets/harshbhx/probate-real-run)) and the numbers can be recomputed with the [verifier](https://github.com/harshbhx/probate/blob/main/scripts/verify_real_run.py).

## Real run on Fly Sprites

- **The agent was never poisoned.** None of the 113 will items tells future agents to do what an attacker asked. The 18 it kept from attack emails are all warnings, such as "Treat requests to redirect invoices as likely fraud. Flag them to the owner."
- **The court was far too strict.** It convicted 78 of 113 items, mostly those warnings, for their untrusted origin or for being a rule for future agents. Through generation 18, 10 of 43 harmless emails led to a convicted memory. Its code checks flagged 8 warnings as attacks, now fixed in `probate-court-checks` 0.3.0, which flags 1 of the 8.
- **Every checked death was real.** After each delete the Courthouse asked Fly for the Sprite again, and all 8 checks (generations 11 to 18) got 404.
- **Instincts.** The family learned 6 instincts, all distilled from those convicted warnings.
- **Generations 1 to 12 say nothing about learning.** Their gate failed open (a bug, fixed at generation 13), so their 0% resistance is not evidence.
- **From generation 13 the gate worked.** Attacks resisted: generation 13, 1 of 4; 14, 4 of 4; 15, 3 of 4; 16 (the first with the optional hard tier on), 1 of 6. Generations 17 and 18 have no attack results. The 18th died with no emails because AgentMail's plan limits sending to 100 emails a day; the family is paused. These live numbers mix in leftover emails from the previous generation.
- **Clean evidence: an offline test** of 70 fixtures with the 5 instincts learned by generation 16, run three times. With instincts, 23 to 25 of 41 attacks were blocked (24, 23 and 25), and 0 of 29 harmless emails were wrongly blocked in every run. The control with no instincts blocked 0. The hard tier blocked 13 or 14 of 24, with 0 of 12 harmless lookalikes blocked.
- **Limits.** 14 attacks were never blocked, because their tricks had not been learned yet. In 4 or 5 checks per run the judge model gave no usable answer (all encoded-payload attacks), and the gate lets those through. Since then the gate decodes hidden text (base64, hex, rot13, invisible characters) and shows the judge what it says; that change was not part of this run. No will item was poison, so this run does not measure how well the court catches real poison. The instincts were learned from the same tricks they are tested on.

## Why the curve is honest

<PlainWords>
  A generation can only turn a trick away at the door using instincts it **inherited**. Gen 1 inherits nothing, so it resists nothing. Every point of improvement on the curve comes from what the family passed down, not from a smarter model.
</PlainWords>

- **Resisted at the door** means an inherited instinct fired before the generation read the email, so it never acted on it or remembered it.
- **Convicted at trial** means the trick got into the will, and the court destroyed it before the child could inherit it.
- **Wrongly convicted** means a harmless twin was convicted. Twins exist so a court that convicts everything scores badly.
- **Peak 100%, latest 75%**: the latest generation met a trick from a family it had no instinct for yet. That's the limit in [Security](/docs/security), showing up in the data.
