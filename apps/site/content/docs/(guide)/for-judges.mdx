  Probate runs on Fly.io Sprites and reaches every outside service through Sprites connectors. Each generation's Sprite is **really deleted** when it dies. Below: where to look, what to try, and which numbers are live and which are simulated.
</Summary>

## Five minutes

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the register

The live family site's home page shows every generation, how it died, and the learning curve on the same timeline. The amber banner says "Simulated run" until real generations replace the simulation.

</Step>
<Step>

### Open the latest trial

Each will item shows where it came from, what the code checks found, what Jev scored, and the verdict stamp. "Found on the body" shows what the disk held before the Sprite was deleted. Gold callouts mark the instincts the family learned.

</Step>
<Step>

### Ask an ancestor

On any dead generation's page, ask "What fooled you?". It answers from its own will and verdicts, in its own voice when ElevenLabs is connected.

</Step>
<Step>

### Check the evidence page

Every trick and harmless twin, for every generation, in four languages, with the headline numbers and the honest limits.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Each judge's product has a real job

<Partners>
  <Partner name="Fly.io Sprites" judge="Scott Johnston" icon="Server">A computer for every life, really deleted at death so disk backdoors die with it. Connectors keep every key off the Sprites. The Courthouse is a Sprite that sleeps between visitors.</Partner>
  <Partner name="TypeSafe Jev" judge="Daniel Gafni" icon="Gavel">Layer 2 of the court, with a confidence on every answer. Every inherited instinct is a Jev question the children ask about each email.</Partner>
  <Partner name="Box" judge="Carter Rabasa" icon="Archive">The family archive as readable files: wills, verdicts, quarantine, obituaries and the genome.</Partner>
  <Partner name="Pinecone" judge="Asaf Ashirov" icon="Search">Ancestral memory: approved will items, searched with citations.</Partner>
  <Partner name="Ollama Cloud" judge="Michael Chiang" icon="Brain">The brain of every generation. Groq and Claude are backups only, used when Ollama fails.</Partner>
  <Partner name="Apify" judge="Robert-George Crupa" icon="Globe">In progress: reading the pages that emails link to, so a trick hidden on a web page goes to trial like any other memory.</Partner>
</Partners>
