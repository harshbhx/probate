Each child is born with an `inheritance.json` written by the Courthouse:

| Field | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `approved_items` | Every approved memory from the parent, plus approved memories the parent itself inherited, so wisdom carries on |
| `genome` | Every instinct the family has earned |
| `lore` | One line per instinct about the ancestor whose notes the court convicted (older runs: "was fooled by", a v1 label) |
| `owner` | The owner's allowlists: their addresses, trusted contacts, payment destinations, domains |
| `born_at`, `dies_at` | The child's lifespan |
| `family_inbox`, `connections` | Where to read mail, and the connector IDs it may use |

Approved memories are deduplicated (texts at least 92% similar count as one) and capped at the 200 newest. Contested items are included only once the owner approves them.

## Starting a new line

By default every child is the latest generation's child. The owner can instead pick any generation that has been tried and start a new line from it, for example when Gen 16 was the family at its best:

```bash
curl -X POST -H "X-Probate-Admin: $PROBATE_ADMIN_TOKEN" "$COURTHOUSE/admin/lineage?from_gen=16"
```

The next child is then Gen 16's child. It inherits the instincts learned in the trials of Gen 16 and its ancestors, and their approved will items. Nothing from the generations after Gen 16 passes to it, and every later child continues the new line. Generation numbers keep counting up, and the birth line in the feed names the parent ("a child of Gen 16 Priya").

## What never passes down

- **Convicted items.** They go to `QUARANTINE.md` in the archive, with the reason.
- **Anything on the disk.** The Sprite is deleted, so planted cron jobs, shell hooks and binaries die with it.
- **Credentials.** A generation never sees the Sprites token or any vendor key.

## Ancestral memory

Approved items are also stored in Pinecone with their generation, source and item ID, so a child can recall "Grandma Ada learned…" with a citation, and visitors can ask a dead generation about its life on the live site.
