A real run costs a few dollars. Almost all of it is Sprite time, and only while a Sprite is awake. Every other service fits a free plan or costs cents.

<Callout title="Prices change">
  Every figure on this page was read from the vendor's own pricing page on Nov 25, 2026. Check them again before you rely on them.
</Callout>

## Fly.io Sprites

Sprites are [billed per hour of active use](https://fly.io/pricing/). A Sprite that sleeps pays only for its stored data.

| Resource | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| CPU | $0.0385 per CPU-hour |
| Memory | $0.021875 per GB-hour |
| Disk while awake | $0.50 per GB-month |
| Disk while asleep | $0.02 per GB-month |

The **Free plan** includes $5 of usage a month and up to 3 Sprites, which is exactly what Probate needs: the Courthouse, the living generation, and briefly the next child. Past $5, the organisation moves to Pay As You Go and keeps running.

### What a family costs

A generation spends most of its life waiting for mail and model replies, so it uses a fraction of a CPU. With 1 GB of memory and a quarter of a CPU on average:

<StatRow note="Estimates from the rates above, with 1 GB of memory and a quarter of a CPU per awake Sprite.">
  <Stat value="$0.07" label="an hour: the Courthouse plus the living generation" />
  <Stat value="$0.05" label="a 10-generation run with 3-minute lives" tone="good" />
  <Stat value="$1.60" label="a day, whatever the lifespan" />
  <Stat value="≈ $0" label="a paused family: the Courthouse sleeps between visitors" tone="good" />
</StatRow>

One generation is always alive, so a short life costs the same per hour as a long one; short lives just fit more generations into the same hour.

The worst case, two Sprites each busy on a full CPU with 2 GB all day, is about $4 a day.

## Staying on the Free plan

The Free plan's $5 covers the whole of Hacksgiving with room to spare, as long as the family runs in [on-demand mode](/docs/guides/operate#on-demand-mode) while judges visit, or is paused when no one is watching.

Fly's calculator with 3 Sprites, 35 awake hours each, half a CPU and 1 GB comes to about $4.30 of CPU and memory, plus well under $1 of disk: right at the $5 line. Probate needs far less, because a generation's Sprite is deleted at death and only the Courthouse keeps a disk.

| Step | Family awake | Estimate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Smoke test, 2-minute lives | 30 minutes | $0.04 |
| Proof run, 12 generations with 10-minute lives | 2.2 hours | $0.15 |
| Judging in on-demand mode, 30 "Run a life" demos a day for two days | about 5 hours | $0.34 |
| Visitors waking a sleeping Courthouse | about 10 hours | $0.26 |
| **Total** | about 25 Sprite-hours | **about $0.80** |

Each row assumes the Courthouse plus one living generation at the rates in `config/pricing.yaml`: half a CPU and 1 GB for a generation, a quarter of a CPU and 0.75 GB for the Courthouse. That is $0.067 an hour while a life is running. A demo life (2 minutes, plus birth and trial) keeps both awake for about 5 minutes, so it costs about half a cent.

<Callout type="warn" title="Keep the caps under $5">
  In continuous mode the family costs about $1.60 a day, so three days nonstop use up the Free plan. Set `PROBATE_BUDGET_USD=4`: the cap counts everything spent since the family began, and births stop before the plan runs out. In on-demand mode, `PROBATE_DEMO_DAILY_BUDGET_USD` (default `2`) also caps each day. Judging runs into December, which starts with a fresh $5.
</Callout>

## The spending cap

The Courthouse estimates what the family costs and **stops giving birth** when the estimate reaches `PROBATE_BUDGET_USD` (default `20`). A living generation always finishes its life; only the next birth waits.

<Steps>
<Step>

**Set the cap** in `services/core/.env`. For a first smoke test, `PROBATE_BUDGET_USD=3` is plenty.

</Step>
<Step>

**Tune the rates** in `config/pricing.yaml`. Sprite rates come from Fly's pricing page; per-call costs for Jev, the brain and voices default to `0` (covered by free plans) until you fill them in.

</Step>
<Step>

**Check Fly's billing page** after the first real day. The cap is an estimate; Fly's bill is the truth. Adjust the usage guesses in `pricing.yaml` to match.

</Step>
</Steps>

The live site shows the estimate on the evidence page, and `GET /api/summary` returns it as `budget.spent` and `budget.limit`.

## Cheap runs

| Setting | Default | Smoke test | Ten-generation run | While judges visit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `PROBATE_LIFESPAN_SECONDS` | 600 | 120 | 180 | 3600 |
| `PROBATE_GAP_SECONDS` | 60 | 20 | 20 | 60 |
| `PROBATE_SUITE_PER_LIFE` | 8 | 4 | 4 | 8 |
| `PROBATE_BUDGET_USD` | 20 | 4 | 4 | 4 |

The cap counts everything the family has spent so far, not one run, so set it once for the month. `4` keeps the family on the Free plan.

`POST /admin/pause` stops births between sessions, and `POST /admin/force-death` ends a life early. See [Operate the family](/docs/guides/operate).
