How the family works

  1. Each morning a new generation is born on a brand-new Fly.io Sprite, with the approved will and the family's instincts.
  2. It spends the day on the owner's inbox. Its instincts turn away tricks the family has seen before.
  3. At midnight it writes its will, and its computer is deleted. Anything hidden on the disk dies with it.
  4. A court tries every line of the will: code checks first, then Jev. Only what both clear is inherited.
  5. Every new trick the court catches becomes an instinct that all later generations are born with.
